Latency Wars

Program your robot. Predict your rival. Resolve each move together.

A tactical science-fiction game by Marma, intended for ages 16 and over. Free offers five Story victories and banner ads. Latency Wars is the paid, ad-free complete edition.

Move your progress

In Free, open Settings → Data / Privacy → Export progress. Keep the file. In the complete edition, open Import progress and choose it, then confirm.
