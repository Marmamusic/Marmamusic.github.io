Latency Wars — Privacy notice

Publisher: Marma · Effective 11 November 2026
Support: marma.developer@gmail.com

This notice covers Latency Wars Free (com.marma.latencywars.free) and Latency Wars (com.marma.latencywars), intended for people aged 16 and over.

GAME DATA
Your campaign, earned equipment, manual saves and settings are stored on your device. Marma operates no game account, cloud-save service or gameplay analytics server. Android/Google Play may separately process installation, purchase, backup and diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings.

PROGRESS FILES
Export and import use Android's file picker. The game accesses only the file you choose; it does not request access to all your files. A progress file contains game state and earned unlocks, not a name, email or advertising identifier. Your chosen storage provider may upload that file under its own policy. Delete exported files through that provider when you no longer need them. Imports replace Story progress after confirmation, merge earned Duel unlocks and keep a local pre-import recovery backup.

ADVERTISING IN FREE
Only Free includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Banner requests may transmit IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers to Google for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. A teen-protection signal is sent for ages 16–17. Your selected age band is saved locally; we do not ask for your birth date. People selecting under 16 can continue without starting advertising. The paid app contains neither advertising SDK nor banner ads.

AD CHOICES
Where required, Google's privacy message appears before ads can be requested. Settings > Data / Privacy > Ad choices reopens Google's privacy choices when Google requires this entry point. Choices and local requirements determine which ads can be served. Advertising can be unavailable offline, before consent is resolved, or when no ad is available; gameplay still works. Manage the Android advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings. Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
Full offers optional Bluetooth play. If you grant Nearby devices permission, paired-device names/addresses are used to connect to the opponent you choose. Match setup and actions travel directly between those devices; Marma receives no match data.

RETENTION AND DELETION
Game files remain locally until overwritten, cleared in Android app settings or removed on uninstall, subject to Android backup/restore behavior. Exported files and local recovery backups are separate copies. Clearing an app's storage removes its local progress and settings; keep an export if you want to restore progress. To request help or deletion of information you sent by email, contact marma.developer@gmail.com. Marma cannot delete data held independently by Google or your chosen document provider; use their privacy controls.

THIS WEBSITE
These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Website visits are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

CHANGES
If this notice changes, its effective date will change. Contact Marma with questions about these practices.
