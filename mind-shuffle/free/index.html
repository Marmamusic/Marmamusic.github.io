Mind Shuffle Free — Privacy

Updated 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Free Android edition, package com.marmamusic.mindshuffle.free.

Developer: marma
Contact: marma.developer@gmail.com.

Your game and audio

Progress, your unfinished round, selected level and mute preference stay on your device. The game has no player account, cloud progress service, microphone access or developer-operated analytics. Music and soft tap sounds play locally. The speaker button mutes both.

Advertising and consent

The Free Android edition includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google's SDKs may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Ads need a network connection; the memory game itself works offline.

The app refreshes consent information at launch and waits until Google's consent system permits ad requests before initializing advertising. Required consent forms appear through Google's tools. When available, the native Ad privacy button lets you reopen advertising privacy choices. Ads appear outside rounds, never over the board.

See Google's Privacy Policy and the Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google's processing and retention information. The browser and standalone HTML previews do not include the native advertising SDK.

Advertising privacy choices

When available, the native Ad privacy button lets you reopen Google’s privacy options. You can also mute the app with the speaker button. The game remains playable when you are offline or an ad cannot load. Google controls retention of data processed through its services under its policies.

Storage and your choices

Local game settings and progress remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. You can mute audio in the app or erase local data through Android’s app settings. Removing local data does not cancel a purchase recorded by Google Play.

This website

These privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Website visits are covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. The offline Android app is separate from this website and from the privately hosted browser preview.
