Drifting Words Free privacy policy

Developer: marma
Updated 30 October 2026
Contact: marma.developer@gmail.com

This policy covers Drifting Words Free, Android package com.marmamusic.driftingwords.free. Free includes 30 Easy and 30 Medium drifts with banner advertising. Higher difficulties are unavailable in this edition.

Local game data and audio

Progress, selected difficulty, an unfinished puzzle and the sound preference are saved on your device. There is no player account, cloud progress service or developer analytics backend. Music and tap sounds play locally. The app does not record the microphone, collect audio files or access health records. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local game data. Free and Full are separate apps and keep separate progress; there is no automatic transfer between them.

Advertising in Free

Free uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. The advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses that can indicate approximate location, device or account identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app requests consent information before requesting ads as permitted by the consent state. Where required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Banners appear on home and result screens, not during an active puzzle.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google controls retention and account-related rights for the data it processes. Clearing this app's local data does not delete records held by Google. Use Google's privacy and account controls where applicable.

Separate Full app

Free has no in-app purchase, subscription or purchase-restoration service. Its Full button opens the separate paid app's Google Play listing. Full includes all 720 drifts, has no advertising SDK and has its own privacy policy. Installing Full does not remove advertising from Free or delete data previously processed by Google's advertising services.

Audience

Drifting Words is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It has no chat, uploads or public player profiles.
