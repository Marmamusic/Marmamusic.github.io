Drifting Words Full privacy policy

Developer: marma
Updated 30 October 2026
Contact: marma.developer@gmail.com

This policy covers Drifting Words Full, Android package com.marmamusic.driftingwords. Full is a separate paid download with all 720 drifts. It has no advertising, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or subscription.

Local game data and audio

Progress, selected difficulty, an unfinished puzzle and the sound preference are saved on your device. There is no player account, cloud progress service or developer analytics backend. Music and tap sounds play locally. The app does not record the microphone, collect audio files or access health records. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local game data. Free and Full are separate apps and keep separate progress; there is no automatic transfer between them.

Data collection in Full

The Full app does not collect or transmit personal data to marma or a developer server. It has no Android Internet permission and does not include Google Mobile Ads, the User Messaging Platform or Google Play Billing. Puzzle play and music work offline. The privacy-policy button opens this public policy in an external browser, which processes the visit under its own settings.

Google Play purchase

The download price and payment are handled by Google Play outside the game. The app does not request a purchase token, payment-card details or purchase restoration. Google processes store and payment information under Google's Privacy Policy. Clearing local progress does not cancel a store purchase. The same eligible Google Play account can reinstall the app under Google Play's rules.

Your choices

Local progress and preferences remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. There is no player account or developer-held game-data record to delete. Buying Full does not delete advertising records associated with a separate installation of Free.

Audience

Drifting Words is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It has no chat, uploads or public player profiles.

Contact

For privacy questions contact marma at marma.developer@gmail.com.

This website

These public policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Visits are covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. This website is separate from offline game play.
