This policy covers the Drifting Words Android app, its free puzzles and optional one-time Full purchase.

Local game data and audio

Progress, selected difficulty, an unfinished puzzle and the sound preference are saved on your device. The app has no player account, cloud progress service or developer analytics backend. Music and tap sounds play locally. The app does not record your microphone or collect audio files. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local game data.

Advertising

Free includes 30 Easy and 30 Medium puzzles with banner advertising. Google Mobile Ads collects and shares IP addresses that may indicate approximate location, device or account identifiers, user product interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This transmission uses TLS. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads as permitted by the consent state. Ad privacy choices can be reopened in Settings when provided for your region. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads disclosure.

Full purchase and restoration

Google Play processes the optional one-time Full purchase. The app receives purchase status and verification information, checks the signature locally, and acknowledges eligible purchases using a purchase token through Google Play. It keeps a local entitlement for offline play and checks ownership during restoration. No developer server receives the purchase token, and the app does not receive or store payment-card details. Full includes all 720 puzzles and removes new in-app banner requests; the ten-puzzle difficulty progression still applies. Previous advertising records held by Google are not deleted by buying Full.

Retention and your choices

Local progress and sound settings remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. Removing local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase. You can restore Full through Settings using the same eligible Google Play account. Google controls retention and account-related rights for the data it processes under its policies. This app has no separate deletion service for Google's advertising records; contact the developer for privacy questions and use Google's privacy and account controls where applicable.

Audience

Drifting Words is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It has no chat, uploads or public player profiles.
