Drifting Words privacy policy

Developer: marma
Updated 30 October 2026
Contact: marma.developer@gmail.com

Drifting Words is available as two separate Android apps. Free, package com.marmamusic.driftingwords.free, contains 30 Easy and 30 Medium drifts with banner advertising. Full, package com.marmamusic.driftingwords, is a paid download containing all 720 drifts with no advertising SDK. There is no in-app Full unlock or subscription in either app.

Local game data and audio

Progress, selected difficulty, an unfinished puzzle and the sound preference are saved on your device. There is no player account, cloud progress service or developer analytics backend. Music and tap sounds play locally. The app does not record the microphone, collect audio files or access health records. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local game data. Free and Full are separate apps and keep separate progress; there is no automatic transfer between them.

Free advertising

Free uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Advertising can process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. The app requests consent information before ads and offers advertising privacy choices in Settings where required. See the Free policy for details.

Full privacy

Full has no Internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or in-app billing. It does not collect or transmit personal data to marma. Google Play handles payment for the download outside the game. See the Full policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Audience

Drifting Words is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It has no chat, uploads or public player profiles.

Contact

For privacy questions contact marma at marma.developer@gmail.com.
