Source-label interpretation

All non-empty tension_content labels are retained literally. Horizon Worlds includes positive_feedback in this field, so membership in the field alone does not establish that a label denotes a substantive conflict. Review source messages before interpreting those labels; no additional recoding was performed.

Reading the Decentraland boundaries

The default shared-topic tension graph has Q=0.091. Its three display communities have weak separation relative to the configuration reference. Broad tensions frequently overlap across the same discussions. Use the groups as navigation and compare their links, anchors and message evidence; do not treat the three colours as proof of three distinct substantive themes. There is no universal modularity pass mark. The other rules and two-topic setting are sensitivity views.
