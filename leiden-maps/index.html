Published 30 October 2026. These are the latest v3 descriptor interfaces, with fixed and animated views, all three connection rules, support thresholds of two or three topic groups, community profiles, full-screen viewing and high-resolution PNG exports. The automated maps use the revised consolidated LLM JSON files. Every viewer is self-contained.

The maps describe computed descriptor communities. Consult the methodology and stability results before developing qualitative interpretations. Manual maps retain their main/contextual classifications; all automated descriptor labels are candidates because a separate LLM main/contextual split was not supplied.
