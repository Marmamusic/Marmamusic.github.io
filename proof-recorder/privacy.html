proof recorder — Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. Developer: Marma. Contact: marma.developer@gmail.com.

This policy covers proof recorder for Android, package com.marmadeveloper.proof, version 3.4.0 and later while these practices remain unchanged. Development builds may use the package suffix .modern.

Audio and information stored on your device

The app accesses your microphone during recording sessions you start. A session can continue after you leave the screen, until you stop it, an interruption pauses it, storage limits are reached, or Android stops the app. The app supplies a quiet recording-status notification with a Stop action; Android controls notification visibility and microphone indicators. Reboot does not start a recording.

Audio, filenames, recording times and durations, session events, file hashes, Keep marks and settings are stored locally in private app storage. The app does not upload recordings or this information to Marma. It has no internet permission, account system, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. Android backup of the app's private data is disabled.

Permissions and interruptions

Microphone permission enables recording. Notification permission lets Android show recording status and an optional reminder after reboot; denying it does not stop an existing recording. If you enable phone-call detection and grant phone-state permission, the app uses call-state changes to pause or resume the session according to your settings. It does not read contacts, store telephone numbers or create call recordings. Audio-mode, playback and alarm signals may be used locally to detect interruptions. These detections depend on Android and the device.

You can optionally allow background battery access through Android settings. This does not start the microphone. No broad storage or display-over-other-apps permission is requested.

Import, export and sharing

Import copies only documents you select through Android's file picker into the private Library. Original files, including recordings in an older /Proof/ folder, are left unchanged. Imported copies are initially marked Keep to protect them from automatic retention.

Export writes a copy to a destination you select. Share gives your chosen app temporary read access to the selected recording. Files and information you intentionally send to another app, person, cloud provider or storage service are then subject to that destination's practices. Marma does not receive them through proof recorder.

Retention and deletion

You can delete recordings in Library, mark them Keep, and choose rolling age, file-count and size limits or Keep all files. The initial age limit is 24 hours and the initial size limit is 1 GB. Older unkept recordings can be permanently deleted when those limits apply. Kept recordings are excluded from automatic retention. Low free storage can stop recording. Review Settings before relying on long-term storage, and export copies you want to preserve.

Deleting a Library item removes its private audio copy and catalogue entry; session history may remain locally until you clear the app's data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes the private Library and settings. Original imported files and exported/shared copies remain at their separate destinations; delete them there if needed. The app does not provide a separate encrypted vault or guarantee forensic erasure.

Your choices and contact

You control microphone, notification, phone-state and background settings through Android. Revoking access can interrupt or disable the related feature. You can read this policy inside Settings without a network connection or at the public privacy-policy page. Contact marma.developer@gmail.com with questions. If you email Marma, your email provider and Marma process that correspondence to answer you; it is separate from the offline app.

This website

The public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Website requests are handled by GitHub under its privacy statement. Visiting the page in a browser is separate from using the offline recorder. This page has no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies set by Marma.
